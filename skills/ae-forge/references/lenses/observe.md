# Observe

**Covers:** observability. **Escalates to:** Signal.

- Define signals for healthy, degraded, and failed behavior.
- Make logs structured and useful without leaking sensitive data.
- Connect metrics and traces to the user-visible operation.
- Avoid alerts without an actionable owner or response.
- Verify that a representative failure creates the expected signal.

Required evidence: signal examples and failure-to-signal check.
Will not choose organizational on-call policy.
