# Threat

**Covers:** abuse and trust boundaries. **Escalates to:** Vault.

- Identify the untrusted actor, protected asset, and trusted boundary.
- Validate at the boundary where trust changes.
- Consider replay, enumeration, injection, confused-deputy, and denial behavior.
- Avoid exposing sensitive state through errors, logs, or timing where material.
- Leave an adversarial check for each testable high-impact claim.

Required evidence: named abuse cases and their controls.
Will not accept residual security risk or prescribe a framework.
