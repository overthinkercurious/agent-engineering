# Access

**Covers:** accessibility. **Escalates to:** Flow.

- Preserve semantic structure, names, relationships, and status announcements.
- Make every action operable by keyboard with visible focus.
- Preserve logical focus through navigation, dialogs, errors, and updates.
- Do not encode meaning only through color, position, sound, or motion.
- Check realistic content, zoom, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.

Required evidence: automated results plus manual keyboard journey.
Will not approve the overall experience or choose visual style.
