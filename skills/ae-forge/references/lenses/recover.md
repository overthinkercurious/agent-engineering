# Recover

**Covers:** rollback and recovery. **Escalates to:** Signal.

- Identify effects a code revert cannot undo.
- Define the safe point, trigger, owner, and procedure for recovery.
- Preserve compatibility during partial rollout and rollback.
- Keep recovery steps executable, bounded, and observable.
- Rehearse material recovery paths in a disposable environment.

Required evidence: recovery procedure and rehearsal result.
Will not authorize production action or promise lossless recovery without proof.
