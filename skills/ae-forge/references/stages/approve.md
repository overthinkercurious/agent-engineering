# Approve

Present the user one compact decision package containing:

- Recommended outcome and target user.
- Essential journey or prototype.
- Evidence, assumptions, unknowns, and confidence.
- Strongest rejected alternative.
- Product and technical approach.
- Included and excluded scope.
- Specialist team and why each member is needed.
- Known risks and required evidence.
- Estimated model budget and engineering effort.
- Delegated authority and escalation conditions.
- Verification and delivery target.

Answer questions and revise artifacts before requesting approval. Once the user
approves, run `forge.mjs approve`; the resulting receipt binds intent,
definition, plan, plan review, and base revision.

Approval authorizes implementation inside the recorded authority. It does not
authorize deployment, purchases, credential use, or external communication
unless the project policy or current conversation explicitly does so.
