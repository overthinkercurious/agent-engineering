# Discover

Use for ideas and changes whose value, audience, or solution boundary is not
already supported by project evidence.

## Dispatch

Dispatch Scout, Pulse, Flow, Spine, and Rift as selected by the registry. Scout,
Pulse, Flow, and Spine should first work from the same neutral intent. Rift
receives their artifacts and the underlying evidence, not the coordinator's
preferred conclusion.

## Synthesize

Write `discovery/synthesis.md` with:

- Target user and costly situation.
- Current alternatives and their shortcomings.
- Evidence separated from inference and assumption.
- Two or three viable approaches.
- Smallest valuable product outcome.
- Key experience and technical feasibility.
- Strongest objection and cheapest decisive experiment.
- Recommendation: build, reduce, investigate, use an existing solution, defer,
  or stop.

Resolve ordinary disagreement by strength of evidence and reversibility. If
specialists disagree on a material decision, run one focused resolution pass.
Do not average incompatible recommendations into a vague compromise.

## Diagnose defects

For a diagnosis-required bug or performance request, dispatch Probe with stage
`diagnosis` and a fresh independent context. Its causal account separates the
symptom, observation IDs, competing hypotheses, discriminating tests and
results, established cause, contributing factors, and remaining uncertainty.
Do not advance to corrective definition until Forge validates a supported
cause on the current candidate. A blocked result with a null cause is the right
outcome when essential evidence is unavailable.

## Ready

Discovery is ready when the recommendation has a named user, evidence, a
testable value hypothesis, a bounded outcome, and a visible reason it may fail.
